What SmartPlay Caddie does

A Caddie That Talks Back

Kevin and Serena talk you through the shot — distance, club, target, and the miss you can live with. Or build a caddie of your own and set the voice yourself.

Shot Tracking Made Simple

Hole-by-hole scoring and strokes logged as you play, with your round measured against the last one so you can see what actually changed.

SmartMotion Swing Analysis

SmartMotion reads your swing from your phone camera — or a clip from your GoPro or tablet — and names the fault, then gives you one thing to work on, not a lecture.

Phone, Tablet and Watch

Yardages and your round on your Apple Watch or Wear OS watch. A bigger screen on your tablet at the range. Simulator lessons from SmartBay AI in your practice history. One caddie, almost anywhere you play.

What you get on the first tee

GPS yardages to the front, middle, and back. A club call that accounts for wind, elevation, and lie. SmartMotion swing analysis from your phone camera. All of it spoken, so your hands stay on the club.
