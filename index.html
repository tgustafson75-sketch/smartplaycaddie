Meet your caddie

A caddie, not another golf app

Most golf apps hand you numbers and leave the decision to you. SmartPlay Caddie gives you the decision: the club, the target, and the miss to favor — spoken while you're standing over the ball. Kevin and Serena caddie for you out of the box, and you can build your own if you'd rather.

Works with what you already have

One caddie. Almost anywhere you play.

The course, the range, the backyard net, the simulator bay. Your phone, your watch, your tablet, your camera. It all feeds the same caddie — so it knows your game no matter where you played it.

On your wrist

Apple Watch and Wear OS. Your yardages and your round come straight from your phone to your watch, so you can glance instead of reaching for your pocket.

Phone or tablet

Run SmartPlay Caddie on your phone on the course, or on a tablet at the range and in the bay where a bigger screen makes sense.

Bring your own camera

Film your swing on a GoPro or a tablet and import the clip. Tag it face-on or down-the-line and SmartMotion reads it like it was shot in the app.

Data from the bay

Hitting in a simulator? Lessons from SmartBay AI land in your practice history, so your caddie knows what you worked on before you get to the first tee.

Your golf tech doesn't know you. Or each other.

Your GPS app tracks distance. Your swing analyzer tracks motion. Your rangefinder measures yardage. Your stats tracker logs scores. Your coaching content delivers tips. Each one does its job — and none of them talk to each other. They don't know your tendencies, your history, or how your game is evolving. They're tools. Not a caddie.
