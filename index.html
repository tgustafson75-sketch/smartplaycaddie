Works everywhere you practice

One caddie. Almost anywhere you play.

On the course

GPS yardages, the club call and the play for every hole — ask out loud, or just type. Your round, score and stats are tracked as you go.

Range, backyard, living room

At the range, in the backyard net, or a hotel room on the road. Your practice sessions feed the same caddie that walks the course with you.

On your wrist or a bigger screen

Apple Watch and Wear OS: yardages and your round come straight from your phone, so you glance instead of reaching for your pocket. Prefer a tablet at the range? That works too.

Bring your own camera

Film your swing on a GoPro or a tablet and import the clip. Tag it face-on or down-the-line and SmartMotion reads it like it was shot in the app.

Data from the bay

Hitting in a simulator? Lessons from SmartBay AI land in your practice history, so your caddie knows what you worked on before the first tee.
