Quick answers

What is an AI golf caddie?

An app that does what a human caddie does: reads the hole, knows your distances, and recommends the club, the target and the miss to favor. SmartPlay Caddie speaks that advice so you never have to look at a screen over the ball.

Do I need sensors or extra hardware?

No. Your phone is the whole kit — GPS for yardages, the camera for swing analysis, and your voice for everything else.
