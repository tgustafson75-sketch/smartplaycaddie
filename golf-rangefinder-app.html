Quick answers

Is SmartPlay Caddie a laser rangefinder?

No — it's a GPS rangefinder app. Distances come from your phone's GPS and the course map, so there's nothing extra to carry or charge.

What if my course isn't in the app?

Live yardages need the course geometry, so a missing course won't show front/middle/back numbers yet. Your caddie still talks strategy, and you can email us the course to add.
