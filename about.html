Why we built SmartPlay Caddie

A good caddie does more than read a yardage. They pick a club, name a target, and tell you which miss you can live with — so you can stop deliberating and swing. Almost no one gets to play with one. SmartPlay Caddie is the attempt to put that in your pocket: it knows the hole, factors the conditions, and says the number out loud. Kevin and Serena come with the app, and you can build your own if you'd rather have a different voice in your ear.

What it actually does

Ask, and Get an Answer

Speak to your caddie the way you would to a real one, and hear the number, the club and the target back — without taking your phone out.

A Number You Can Trust

Front, middle and back yardages from GPS, with wind, elevation and the lie factored in — so the number you hear is the number to play.

One Fix at a Time

Record a swing with your phone camera and SmartMotion names the fault — shoulder turn, hip turn, tempo — then gives you one thing to work on instead of ten.
